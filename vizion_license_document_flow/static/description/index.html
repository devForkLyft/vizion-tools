License & Document Flow

Store retail licenses, wholesale licenses and tax ID documents — with file uploads and expiry dates — on every Odoo contact, and carry them straight into Point of Sale.

by Vizion Tools

OVERVIEW

Compliance documents that live where you work

Every retail license, wholesale permit and tax ID — with its file and expiry date — kept on the customer record. No more chasing paperwork across folders and email.

Businesses that sell to licensed retailers, wholesalers and distributors must record and prove each customer’s licenses. License & Document Flow adds dedicated license and document fields directly to the Odoo Contacts form, so your team can capture, store and verify a customer’s compliance documents in one place — and the most important details are loaded into Point of Sale so cashiers see them at the till.

KEY FEATURES

Everything on the customer record

☑  Retail license with file & expiry

Record a retail license number, attach the license file, and set an expiry date so you always know when it needs renewing — right on the contact.

☑  Up to three wholesale licenses

Store Wholesale License 1, 2 and 3 side by side, each with its own uploaded document, for customers that hold multiple permits across states or product lines.

☑  Tax ID document & expiry tracking

Attach the customer’s tax ID file and track its own expiry date, keeping tax documentation together with the rest of the compliance record.

☑  Customer Unique ID / Number

Give each customer a unique identifier for fast lookup, cross-referencing and reporting across sales, invoicing and Point of Sale.

☑  Point of Sale ready

Key license details are loaded into the POS partner data, so the information is available to your cashiers during a sale — no switching back to the back office.

☑  Native Odoo experience

The fields extend the standard contact form — no new screens to learn. Everything appears on the Other Info tab, where your team already works.

WHO IT’S FOR

Built for regulated wholesale & retail

Ideal for tobacco, vape and nicotine distributors, beverage and food wholesalers, and any B2B business that must keep customer licenses and tax documents audit-ready. Works hand in hand with the MSA Report module for Master Settlement Agreement compliance, sharing the customer Class of Trade classification.
